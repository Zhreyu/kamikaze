# Home (`/`): surface brief

Scope: the home page of kamikaze.host. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: ravers arriving from an Instagram link on a phone; artists and venues checking the room is real. Job: say what Kamikaze is, show the next date, get the visitor on the list. Action: `Get the venue first` to `/pass/`. Proof: OVERRIDE, planned for 250, 500 came. Constraints: static export, two base paths, 390x844 first, nothing untrue, no fake data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lights off. The page is the room before the drop: black, one wordmark, one date, one red action. It refuses the event-site default of a poster carousel, a lineup grid and a ticket widget; a sealed date is the headline.

OWN-WORLD: Void #0a0a0a ground, bone #efefef type, one red (#cc0000 fill, #ff3b30 type). Three voices: the slashed CyberpunkCity display face with a red/cyan split, expanded Archivo 900 for dates and numbers, IBM Plex Mono labels. Clipped-corner red buttons, hairline frames with two hot corner brackets, redacted bars for facts not yet announced, scanline, grain and vignette over everything, a chrome-red 3D sigil behind the content.

STORY: The visitor understands this is a techno collective with a night on Thu 31 Dec 2026, believes it because the first one drew twice its plan, and signs up to hear the venue first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed bar (sigil, Kamikaze, Menu). The one-line wordmark across the full width over the sigil in its ring, the tagline chip under it. Bottom third: the date in red expanded caps at 38px, `Rite 002 / sealed`, sealed City and Venue bars, one line, then the full-width red button. Date and button are visible at 390x844 without scrolling.

FORM: direction pinned by the owner's approved design canvas (Main.dc.html, Home-Desktop.dc.html) and live prototype; no alternatives were rolled. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
